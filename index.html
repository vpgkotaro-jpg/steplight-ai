StepLight

Turn an overwhelming task list into one clear next step.

How to use: 1 paste your tasks and press "Make my list". 2 press "Start" on the task you choose. 3 in Focus, do the one step shown, then press Done. Keys: 1, 2, 3 switch screens; in Focus, D = Done, S = Too big, P = Pause.

Empty your head

Paste anything. Bullets, numbers, [ ] boxes, semicolons and "then" are understood. Words like "fri" or "!!" are noticed. Nothing leaves this browser.

Pick one

A suggested order: urgent first, then anything with a day, then your own order. You choose.
